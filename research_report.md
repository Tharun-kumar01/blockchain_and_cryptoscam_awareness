# Research Report: Blockchain Basics and Crypto/Investment Scam Patterns

**Audience:** College students and young investors | **Date prepared:** 24 Oct 2026
**Method:** AI-assisted web research (Claude with web search) → cross-checked against official/regulator pages → items marked ✅ (primary source seen), 🟡 (seen only via secondary/news coverage), ⚠️ (not verified — check manually).

## 1. Why this audience?
FTC data (reported in news coverage of its 2021–22 data spotlights) found people in their 20s and 30s were heavily represented among those reporting investment-scam losses, and that crypto was a leading payment method in reported losses. 🟡 Young investors are digitally active, follow social-media "finfluencers", and are often first-time investors — ideal scam targets.

## 2. Blockchain fundamentals (summary)
- **Blockchain:** a shared digital ledger where records ("blocks") are linked in order and copied across many computers, so past entries are very hard to alter secretly.
- **Cryptocurrency:** digital asset recorded on such a ledger. Its price is driven by demand and speculation, not by a guarantee.
- **Wallet:** software/hardware holding your *private keys* (proof of ownership), not literally coins.
- **Private key / seed phrase:** the secret that controls your assets. Anyone who has it controls them. No legitimate service needs it.
- **Irreversibility:** transactions generally cannot be reversed — the FTC notes payments typically cannot be reversed once sent. ✅ (FTC consumer advice page)

## 4. Scam patterns found (by recurring theme)
1. **Guaranteed/high returns** – no legitimate investment is guaranteed (FTC).
2. **Impersonation** – celebrities, "officials", exchange staff, fake SEBI registration certificates.
3. **Fake apps/groups** – messaging-app groups pushing a "special" trading app with fake dashboards.
4. **Pig-butchering / romance-investment** – relationship built online → "investment tip" → fake profits → can't withdraw (extra "tax/fee" demanded).
5. **Giveaway scams** – "send X, get 2X back".
6. **Phishing for seed phrases** – fake support, fake wallet "verification".
7. **Rug pulls / exit scams / Ponzi** – promoters vanish or pay old investors with new money.
8. **Pay-to-work / fee-first** – "pay to unlock job/prize/withdrawal".
9. **Urgency and secrecy** – "limited slots", "don't tell your family".

## 5. Reporting channels (verify before publishing)
- India: cybercrime.gov.in (✅ mentioned in secondary source); national helpline **1930** (✅ confirmed in a Ministry of Home Affairs Rajya Sabha reply, 6 Apr 2022: helpline 1930 for reporting financial frauds, linked to cybercrime.gov.in; call quickly, then file on the portal); SEBI SCORES and SEBI Market Intelligence portal mi.sebi.gov.in (🟡); report to your bank immediately.
- US: ReportFraud.ftc.gov (✅).
