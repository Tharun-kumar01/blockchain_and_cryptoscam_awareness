# Fictional Scam Messages — "TRAINING SAMPLES"
> All names, tokens, apps, numbers and links below are **invented**. Any resemblance to a real person, brand, token or platform is coincidental. Never contact any contact detail in a real scam message.

---
## Sample 1 — The Guaranteed-Returns Group Invite
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> Hi! You've been added to "Smart Profit Circle 🚀". Our mentor Ms. Rao's AI bot gives **guaranteed 4% DAILY returns** on ZetaToken. Only 5 slots left today! Download the SureGain app from the link below and deposit ₹10,000 to start. Don't tell anyone, this is a private opportunity.
> hxxp://suregain-example[.]invalid

**Red flags:** unsolicited group · "guaranteed" · unrealistic daily return · urgency · secrecy · app via link · identity not verifiable.
**Safe response:** leave the group, don't download, verify any "advisor" on the regulator's official register, report the message.

---
## Sample 2 — The "Registered Advisor" Certificate
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> Dear investor, I am a government-certified advisor (certificate attached 📎). Join our VIP channel for IPO allotment guarantee and institutional trading account access. One-time fee ₹5,000. Limited seats.

**Red flags:** claims registration (real scammers often name a real regulator) but gives a screenshot, not a verifiable number · "guaranteed allotment" · fee first · exclusivity.
**Safe response:** check the name on the official regulator list; a certificate image proves nothing.

---
## Sample 3 — The Friendly Stranger (Pig-Butchering Style)
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> Hey Aman 😊 sorry, wrong number! But you seem really nice. I'm a nurse abroad, I trade a little in my free time. My uncle taught me a trick on a platform called NovaVault. Want me to show you? I made 3x last month. I'll guide you, don't worry.

*(Later messages)*
> Your balance is ₹1,80,000! To withdraw, please pay 20% "tax" first.

**Red flags:** wrong-number opener · fast friendship · investment advice from a new online contact · fake dashboard profits · fee to withdraw.
**Safe response:** never mix online relationships and investing; stop contact; report.

---
## Sample 4 — The Giveaway
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> 🎉 LIVE NOW: Famous founder "Mr. X" is giving back to the community! Send 0.1 ZetaToken to the address below and receive 0.2 back instantly. First 100 only!
> Address: [FAKE-ADDRESS-NOT-REAL]

**Red flags:** impersonated public figure · "send X get 2X" · countdown · crypto payment. No real giveaway works this way.
**Safe response:** ignore and report the account.

---
## Sample 5 — The Wallet "Security Alert"
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> ⚠ URGENT: Your WalletExample account will be suspended in 2 hours due to unusual activity. Verify now by entering your 12-word recovery phrase at: hxxp://walletexample-verify[.]invalid

**Red flags:** fake urgency · threat · lookalike link · asks for **recovery phrase**.
**Safe response:** nobody legitimate asks for it. Don't click. Open the official app yourself if worried.

---
## Sample 6 — The Recovery Agent (Second Scam)
**[TRAINING SAMPLE]**
> We are a government-approved Crypto Recovery Cell. We saw your complaint online and can recover your lost ₹2,50,000. Pay a 10% "legal clearance fee" today to start. 100% success rate.

**Red flags:** unsolicited · "government-approved" but no verifiable details · advance fee · "100% success" · targets past victims.
**Safe response:** report only through official portals/bank; never pay a stranger to "recover" funds.

---
### Teaching notes
Ask learners to: (1) highlight every red flag, (2) match it to the checklist, (3) write the safest next action.
